title: "Near-identical probabilities, opposite decisions"
author: "Yanxi Feng (yanxif2)"

Two models can give nearly identical estimated probabilities (say 0.48 vs. 0.52 for the same observation) yet make opposite class decisions. Why does a tiny difference in probability matter so much near the cutoff, and what does that say about judging models by accuracy alone?
